# Questions for clinician review

40 questions, drafted from standard anatomy references
(Gray's, Moore, Last's) without a clinician. Please check each stem, answer and
explanation before launch. Friend approved every item on 2026-10-13 (review.status "approved").

Answers are structures in the 3D model of the neck. The model is Z-Anatomy
(CC BY-SA 4.0) with real nerves and vessels on the right side, plus a few
schematic additions. The schematic structures are listed at the end under
"Schematic structures to review", so check those too. Spelling is UK.

## Schematic structures (tools/hn_schematic.py)

The Z-Anatomy head-neck export lacks these structures, so they are drawn as tubes, spheres and
rings whose positions are derived in code from the export's own meshes (right side only for
paired structures), then pushed clear of bone. Diameters are typical values. Please check:

### Neck (source/schematic/neck.glb)
- **Phrenic nerve**: from the lateral border of scalenus anterior near its upper end (C4/C5), down
  its anterior surface (1.5 mm in front of the muscle) obliquely to its medial border, behind the
  subclavian vein, ending on the lateral side of the right brachiocephalic vein about 1.3 cm below
  the first rib.
- **Ansa cervicalis**: superior root leaves the hypoglossal nerve where it crosses the carotid sheath,
  runs down the front of the sheath (between the internal jugular vein and the carotid) to the level
  where omohyoid crosses the vein (about the cricoid), loops, and the inferior root climbs on the
  anterolateral surface of the vein to the C2/C3 level.
- **Recurrent laryngeal nerve**: leaves the vagus at the subclavian artery, hooks under the artery and
  climbs in the groove between trachea and oesophagus to enter the larynx behind the right
  cricothyroid joint. (The export's vagus lies behind the artery origin, not in front of it.)
- **Superior laryngeal nerve**: from the vagus at the C2 level, down and medial behind the internal
  carotid artery to the tip of the greater horn of the hyoid, where it divides.
- **Internal laryngeal nerve**: forward from the division to pierce the thyrohyoid membrane between the
  hyoid and the upper border of the thyroid cartilage, about 1 cm in front of the greater horn.
- **External laryngeal nerve**: down on the inferior pharyngeal constrictor along the posterior border
  of the thyroid lamina to the cricothyroid muscle.
- **Superior thyroid artery**: from the front of the external carotid just above the bifurcation,
  forward and down beside the external laryngeal nerve to the upper pole of the right thyroid lobe.
- **Carotid sinus**: a 4 mm tube with a 4.5 mm bulb over the first 8 mm of the internal carotid artery
  from the bifurcation.
- **Great auricular nerve**: from Erb's point (posterior border of sternocleidomastoid at C3/C4) up
  obliquely across the lateral surface of the muscle (1.5 mm superficial) to the lower pole of the
  parotid gland.
- **Transverse cervical nerve**: from Erb's point forward across sternocleidomastoid to its anterior
  border and beyond, under platysma.
- **Lesser occipital nerve**: from Erb's point up along the posterior border of sternocleidomastoid to
  the occipital bone behind the mastoid.
- **Supraclavicular nerves** (one tube for the fan): from Erb's point down through the posterior triangle
  to the middle third of the clavicle.
- **Greater occipital nerve**: from below the inferior border of obliquus capitis inferior (medial part,
  at the axis), up behind it and rectus capitis posterior major (5 mm behind, in place of semispinalis
  capitis, which the export lacks) to the superior nuchal line, 2 mm off the bone, at the occipital artery.
- **Transverse ligament of the atlas**: an arc from the medial tubercle of one lateral mass of the atlas
  to the other, passing 1 mm behind the dens.

### Head (source/schematic/head.glb)
- **Facial nerve branches**: not drawn; the export's facial nerve already fans out to frontalis,
  orbicularis oculi, buccinator, depressor anguli oris and platysma.
- **Supraorbital nerve**: from the orbit apex above the optic canal forward under the orbital roof
  (2.5 mm below it), out through the supraorbital notch/foramen (where the supra-orbital artery
  crosses the orbital margin) and 3 cm up the forehead between frontal bone and frontalis.
- **Infraorbital nerve**: along the infra-orbital artery (1 mm lateral to it) through the infraorbital
  groove/canal of the maxilla to the infraorbital foramen, then 1.5 cm down onto the cheek.
- **Coronal, sagittal, lambdoid and squamous sutures**: the outer-surface contact lines between frontal
  and parietal, the two parietals, parietal and occipital, and parietal and temporal (right) bones,
  drawn as 1.2 mm ridges raised 0.8 mm off the bone.
- **Bregma / lambda**: where the sagittal suture meets the coronal / lambdoid suture.
- **Pterion**: the point of the right outer skull surface closest to frontal, parietal, temporal and
  sphenoid bones together.
- **Foramen rings**: each ring sits where the named nerve or vessel of the export passes through the
  bone (foramen magnum: the hole of the occipital bone around the medulla; external acoustic meatus:
  3 mm lateral to the tympanic membrane). Ring sizes are typical, not measured.
